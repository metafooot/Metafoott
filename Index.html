🏟️ INCOME ASSET

METAFOOT Stadium
Multiple Ways to Earn

The METAFOOT Stadium is a fixed digital asset – exactly 1,000 exist. It does not rely on bonding curves or speculative pricing. Instead, it functions as a **venue‑based earning vehicle** within the game. Here’s how owners generate $FOOT:

🏠 Hosting & Rental Fees
Owners can list their stadium as a venue for PvP matches and tournament finals. Managers pay a hosting fee in $FOOT to use the stadium, creating a steady stream of passive income for the owner.
🎟️ Ticket Sales (Spectator Mode)
When the game launches, high‑stakes matches will attract spectators. Owners earn a cut from every virtual ticket sold for matches held in their stadium – a revenue share that scales with the popularity of the event.
📢 Sponsorship & Advertising
As the ecosystem grows, stadiums will become prime real estate for in‑game advertising banners. Owners receive a share of the ad revenue generated by their specific venue, tied directly to foot traffic and match volume.
📈 Scarcity‑Driven Demand
With only 1,000 stadiums and potentially millions of players, the demand for premium venues will naturally outpace supply. Owners can set competitive rental prices, and the value of the asset itself is anchored to its utility and historical position in the game world.

Ownership is recorded on the blockchain. These earning mechanisms are designed to be sustainable, game‑native, and accessible to any stadium holder.

🏠 RENTAL & TRAINING

Earn Passively & Build Your Avatar

🏠 Rental system – earn even when you're not playing: Own a team? Rent it out for a fee in $FOOT. The renter controls your team in PvP and tournaments. When the renter wins prize money, it's split 50/50 between the renter and the team's players. The owner already got paid – no extra split.

🧠 Train player – you are the player: Every time you train your avatar (Speed, Shot, Power, Intelligence, Brilliance, Accuracy), you're building your digital football self. The stronger you become, the more managers will want to sign you.

💰 How you earn – fair for everyone: When a manager wins a tournament: 70% of the prize goes to all players signed to that team (shared equally). 30% goes to the manager. In a PvP match with a rented team: the renter and the team players split winnings 50/50.

🔁 Buy and sell player: In Manager Mode, you can buy and sell player avatars on the market – just like real football transfers. This creates a living economy inside the game.
